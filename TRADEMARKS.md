## What is fine without asking

- Sharing **unmodified** copies of the game or of this repository, with the name and icon as they are.
- Saying what your work is based on: “based on Teul”, “a fork of Teul”, “compatible with Teul saves”.
- Writing about the game, reviewing it, streaming it, and using its name and screenshots to refer to it.

## What needs a different name and icon

- A **fork**, and any **modified build** you distribute (a changed APK, a desktop build, a hosted web version with changes), must use a **different name and a different icon**, and must not suggest that it is the official Teul or is endorsed by its authors.
- Changing the application id (`app/capacitor.config.ts`, `desktop/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`) so that your build does not install over the official one.
