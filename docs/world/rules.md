# World-building rules

[Українською](rules.uk.md)

The basic rules that Lypove (Липове) stands on: the town, the maps around it, the interiors and the rift worlds. They are the same for every place and do not depend on who builds it: a person, an agent or a generator. Creativity lies in what we build within these rules, not in breaking them. Most of the rules already hold in the game; a few are principles we follow, even though not every place fully meets them yet.

Units of measure:
- **step** — one step of ground, roughly knee-high on a person;
- **cell** — one tile of ground underfoot;
- **water level** — the surface of a lake or pond; everything above is land, everything below is depth.

---

## 1. Land and heights

1. The land is made of cells, each with its own height in steps.
2. Plains stay plains, but may lie in bands with low steps between them.
3. Hills are gentle, without cliffs.
4. Rocky places are cut into irregular ledges. A cliff is never lower than three steps, so you cannot climb straight up: paths, stairs and ramps lead to the top.
5. Near the town the land joins it smoothly; inside the town there are terraces with retaining walls, stairs and ramps.
6. Every map has edges. The world never breaks off into emptiness: a map ends in forest, field or a road to the neighbouring map.
7. Land below the water level exists only under water or underground (a cave).

## 2. Liquids

8. A liquid always lies flat. One lake or pond has one level over its whole surface; water never stands in steps.
9. A river flows downhill in sections, and each section is level. The river's level changes only where there is a reason: a waterfall, rapids, a dam or a mill weir.
10. Water never stands higher than its banks. Where a bank is lower than the water, there is either a dam or the water spills into a marsh.
11. A marsh is shallow water with tussocks, peat and mud. People cross it on boardwalks and tussocks.
12. Liquid does not flow uphill or hang in the air. The only exception is the magical worlds (rifts), and there the world makes it visible and explains it.
13. Underground water (such as the lake in the cave) also lies flat.

## 3. People

14. A person simply steps up one step.
15. Two steps up takes a hop.
16. More than two steps up cannot be climbed: you need stairs, a path or a ladder.
17. You can jump down from any height, but a fall of six steps or more does damage.
18. A person walks around what blocks them (walls, posts, trees), steps over what is low (a threshold, roots) and passes under what is high (a roof on posts, a tree crown).
19. A person wades through water up to one step deep and swims on the surface where it is deeper. They cannot dive more than one step under water.
20. In water a person moves more slowly than on land.
21. People do not enter private houses — that is a rule of the world. You go only into public buildings (the town hall, shops, the café, the market and so on), your own home, and someone's home when invited.
22. Things in your pockets weigh nothing and do not slow you down.

## 4. Animals

23. Every animal lives where it naturally belongs: a duck on the water, a stork on a wet bank, sheep on the pasture. Not in the forest if it is a steppe animal, and not in the middle of the street.

## 5. Buildings

24. The town is open: no gates, ramparts, palisades or towers. It is defended against nothing.
25. Buildings stand directly on the ground, without slabs underneath.
26. Buildings are "sculpted": varied bases, shapes and glass, not boxes.
27. A building stands on a level plot. If the ground slopes, there is a terrace or a retaining wall under the building, not a building hanging over the slope.
28. Doors open onto somewhere you can reach: a street, a yard, a porch, a boardwalk. There are no doors into a wall, into water or over a drop.
29. Buildings do not stand inside one another and do not block a road.
30. A building on water stands on piles or a pontoon; a landing, pier, bridge and mill are by the water, not in the middle of a field.

## 6. Roads

31. Maps are connected only by ground routes: roads and paths.
32. Roads never lie on top of each other. Where they meet there is a crossroads or a fork with a signpost.
33. A road never runs through rock or water. It goes around rock with switchbacks or stairs, and crosses water by a bridge or a ford.

## 7. General

34. The world is deterministic: the same place at the same time is the same however many times you open it.
35. Coins are never bought for real money.
36. The magical worlds (rifts) may break any of these rules, but only inside themselves and in a way the player can see: things are different here.
37. Oddities and contradictions that do not break the game stay. We fix only what is broken.
